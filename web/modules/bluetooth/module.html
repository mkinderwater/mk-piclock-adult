Pair a device

Start pairing, then open Bluetooth settings on your phone or tablet and select this clock. When numeric confirmation is requested, the same six-digit number is shown on the clock OLED so you can compare it with your phone. Discoverability automatically expires after 120 seconds.

Audio behaviour

Bluetooth and clock audio share the MAX98357A output through a software mixer. This first Bluetooth release permits both sources to play at the same time so pairing and A2DP playback can be validated before source-priority rules are added.
